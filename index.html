About Our Club

TWCP Chess Club has been a staple of the school community for years. We provide a space for players of all skill levels to sharpen their minds and enjoy the game of kings.

Collaborations: We frequently collaborate with other campus organizations including Leo Club, Unite Club, Path Club, Saber, and Robotics to host inter-disciplinary events and community service projects.
