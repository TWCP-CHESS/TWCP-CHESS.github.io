About TWCP Chess Club

Our student-led club brings together chess learners, competitive players, and creative thinkers in a modern, supportive environment.

TWCP provides weekly tactics workshops, friendly matches, and tournament preparation for every level.

Collaborations: Leo Club, Unite Club, Path Club, Saber, Robotics.
